# PocketBioacoustics

Turn your Android phone into a bioacoustics recorder and analyzer.

## 📲 Download App

[<img src="https://shields.io" alt="Download APK" height="45">](https://github.com)

### Installation Steps
1. Tap the **Download PocketBioacoustics APK** button above.
2. Scroll down to **Assets** and tap `PocketBioacoustics.apk`.
3. Open the downloaded file on your Android phone and tap **Install**.
